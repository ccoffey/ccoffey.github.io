Home Office Chair Repair

Aeron Miller chair was falling apart

Disassemble Replace Reassemble

The repair company I found in Dublin wanted €120 just to collect the chair and have a look at it. Estimated repair cost from photos was a minimum of €450 so I decided to repair it myself. I took it apart and replaced the gas canister, seat and lumbar support. Now it is good as new again.
